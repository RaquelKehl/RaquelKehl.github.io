Privacy notice

This digital business card is built privacy-first. It exists to share professional contact details — nothing more.

Data minimisation

The pages at raquelkehl.ch/card and this notice collect no personal data:

  • No cookies
  • No analytics or tracking of any kind
  • No marketing or advertising tools
  • No profiling
  • No third-party embeds — external profiles are plain links

Your display theme preference (dark or light) is stored only in your own browser (localStorage) and is never transmitted anywhere.

The site is served as static files. The hosting provider (GitHub Pages) may process technical connection data, such as IP addresses, in server logs as part of operating the service — see GitHub's privacy statement. This site itself neither sees nor stores that data.

NFC cards and QR codes

Physical NFC cards and printed QR codes store only the address https://raquelkehl.ch/card — no contact details, no identifiers, no tracking parameters. Tapping a card or scanning a code is identical to typing the address yourself.

The downloadable vCard contains only the professional contact details already shown on the card page.
